Mote

A remote for the TVs in your home.

Privacy policy

Mote does not collect, store or share personal data.

  • Mote talks only to TVs and streaming devices on your local Wi-Fi network, to find them and send the buttons you press.
  • Pairing details (the codes and keys your TV issues) are stored in your iPhone's Keychain and never leave your devices.
  • Mote has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no third-party SDKs.
  • Purchases of Mote Pro are handled by Apple. Mote only learns whether Pro is active on your Apple Account.
  • The Apple Watch app sends button presses to your iPhone over Apple's WatchConnectivity; nothing goes to any server.

Effective 25 October 2026. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here.

Support

My TV isn't found. Make sure the TV is on and on the same Wi-Fi network as your iPhone, and that Mote is allowed under Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.

Roku. On the Roku: Settings › System › Advanced system settings › Control by mobile apps › Network access: Enabled.

Samsung. Choose Allow when the TV asks. If you chose Deny, remove Mote under Settings › General › External Device Manager › Device Connection Manager › Device List.

LG. Choose Accept on the TV. To turn the TV on from Mote, enable Settings › General › Devices › TV Management › Turn on via Wi-Fi.

Google TV, Fire TV, Vizio. Type the code the TV shows.
